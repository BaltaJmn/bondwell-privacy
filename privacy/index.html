Bondwell

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Bondwell does not collect your content, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Your bonds

The bonds you add (series, face value or amount, issue date, serial number, owner and note) are stored on your iPhone only. After every change Bondwell also writes a copy of the list to its own folder on your iPhone, visible in the Files app. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include the list, as it does for other apps. You can delete any bond in the app, and everything is removed if you delete the app.

Photos and the camera

Bondwell asks for the camera so it can photograph a paper bond and read its serial number, series, face value and issue date, and lets you pick a photo from your library through the system photo picker, which only gives the app the photo you pick. The text is read on your iPhone. Photos are never uploaded and never saved by Bondwell.

Backups and exports

When you save a backup or export a spreadsheet, you choose where the file goes (iCloud Drive, Files, Mail...). Bondwell does not send it anywhere by itself.

Face ID and reminders

Face ID is handled by iOS: Bondwell only learns whether you were recognised. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Bondwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Bondwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Bondwell, such as your bonds, photos or backups, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Bondwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Bondwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. Bondwell works with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Bondwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: baltajmn@gmail.com. The developer is Baltasar Jiménez Rincón, Spain (details in the legal notice).
