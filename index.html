Bondwell

What your savings bonds are worth today

Bondwell is a savings bond calculator and tracker for iPhone. Add your U.S. Series EE, I and E bonds by hand or from a photo, and see today's value from the redemption tables the Treasury publishes, plus which bonds have stopped earning interest.

No account, no server. Your list stays on your iPhone. Bondwell has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

How it works

Take a photo of a paper bond and Bondwell reads the serial number, series, face value and issue date for you to check, or type them in. Each bond shows its value, the interest so far and the dates that matter: when it can be cashed, when the early-cash penalty ends, when an EE bond doubles and when it stops earning interest. Group bonds by owner, lock the app with Face ID and keep a backup in iCloud Drive or Files.

Where the values come from

The U.S. Treasury publishes savings bond redemption values every May and November. Bondwell ships them inside the app and is updated when new ones come out. Bondwell is not affiliated with the U.S. Department of the Treasury; for electronic bonds, your TreasuryDirect account shows the exact amount.

Price

Your first 3 bonds are free. Bondwell Pro, for unlimited bonds, reminders and spreadsheet export, is a subscription: $9.99 a year or $1.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country). Cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.
